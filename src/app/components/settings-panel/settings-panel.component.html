Help

Page or site? Notes or to-dos?

The tabs on top pick the page or the whole site; the ones below pick its to-dos or its notes. The panel opens on the last To-dos or Notes tab you picked.

Page notes and to-dos show on that page only. Site notes show on every page of the site (a subdomain like blog.example.com is a site of its own). "Move to this site" or "Move to this page", in a note's or to-do's "⋯" menu, switches it between the two.

When is it "the same page"?

A #section, tracking parameters (utm_…, fbclid…), www. and a trailing / don't make a different page. A home page in a language, like /en, is the home page. Other parameters, like YouTube's ?v=…, do make a different page.

Checklists

In a new note, "Checklist" adds a first item; Enter adds the next one. On a saved note, pick "Add checklist" in its "⋯" menu, then use the "Add item" field, and check items off right in the note.

Used to Markdown? Lines like - [ ] item or - [x] done in a note's text become checklist items too.

Reordering notes and to-dos

Drag a note or a to-do by its handle. With the keyboard: Space on the handle picks it up, the arrow keys move it, Space drops it and Escape cancels.

New notes appear on top, and new to-dos at the bottom. Checked to-dos move to "Done", which "Delete all done" empties.

Finding notes on other pages

On the "This site" tab, "Pages on this site" lists the site's pages that have notes or to-dos. Pick one to open it in the current tab.

Deleted something by mistake?

Use "Undo" in the message shown right after deleting a note, a to-do, or all of a list's done to-dos, to-dos or notes. Once that message is gone, there's no trash to restore from.

"Delete all older than", here in Settings, can be undone the same way. "Delete all notes and to-dos" can't.

Text you haven't saved yet

Notes being written and unsaved edits are kept while the panel stays open, even if you switch tabs. Closing the panel discards them.

Syncing between computers

Chrome sync holds about 100 KB in total, and a few hundred pages and sites. Switching moves all your notes, and stopping sync removes the synced copy from every computer.

Why are notes unavailable on some pages?

Only websites can have notes: Chrome's own pages (settings, new tab), files opened from your computer and other browser pages can't.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac) adds or saves, Esc cancels an edit, and Alt+Shift+N opens the panel.

Manage notes and to-dos

Notes by site

Help

Page or site? Notes or to-dos?

The tabs on top pick the page or the whole site; the ones below pick its to-dos or its notes. The panel opens on the last tab you picked.

Click a note or a to-do to edit it (or use its "Edit"); only a to-do's checkbox checks it. In its editor, a note can also get a name, shown above its text.

The arrow beside a note folds it down to its name, or its first line; a to-do list's folds it down to its name and progress. It stays folded until you unfold it.

Page notes and to-dos show on that page only. Site notes show on every page of the site (note: a subdomain is a site of its own). "Move to this site" or "Move to this page", in a note's or to-do's "⋯" menu, switches it between the two.

To-do lists

A to-do can be a list: several things to check off under one name. In the to-dos' field, write the name, press "List", then one item per line (Enter adds the next one); Ctrl+Enter or "Add" adds the list.

Check its items right in the list: it moves to "Done" once they all are. To turn a single to-do into a list, press its "Edit" and add items; remove them all and it's single again.

To put a to-do into a list, pick "Move into a list…" in its "⋯" menu. To get an item out, edit the list and press "Take out" next to it: once saved, it's a to-do of its own, right after the list.

Reordering notes and to-dos

Drag a note or a to-do by its handle. With the keyboard: Space on the handle picks it up, the arrow keys move it, Space drops it and Escape cancels.

New notes appear on top, and new to-dos at the bottom. Checked to-dos move to "Done", which "Delete all done" empties.

Finding notes on other pages

On the "This site" tab, "Pages on this site" lists the site's pages that have notes or to-dos. Pick one to open it in the current tab.

For every site at once, "Manage by site" here in Settings lists each site with notes or to-dos, by last edit or by name.

Deleted something by mistake?

Use "Undo" in the message shown right after deleting a note, a to-do, or all of a list's done to-dos, to-dos or notes. Once that message is gone, there's no trash to restore from.

"Delete all older than" and the deletes in "Manage by site", here in Settings, can be undone the same way.

"Delete all notes and to-dos" can't be undone.

Text you haven't saved yet

Notes being written and unsaved edits are kept while the panel stays open, even if you switch tabs. Closing the panel discards them.

Syncing between computers

${browser === 'firefox' ? 'Firefox' : 'Chrome'} sync holds about 100 KB in total, and a few hundred pages and sites. Switching moves all your notes, and stopping sync removes the synced copy from every computer.

The default settings use local storage by default, which holds around 10 MB in total.

Why are notes unavailable on some pages?

Only websites can have notes: the browser's own pages (settings, new tab), files opened from your computer and other browser pages can't.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac) adds or saves, Esc cancels an edit, and Alt+Shift+N opens the panel.
